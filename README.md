Gobble Run — The Great Thanksgiving Escape, a 2D turkey escape game. You are the turkey, and the farmer wants you for Thanksgiving dinner. Run 1000 metres from the farmyard, through the barn, to the kitchen — and out the other side to the forest gate.

Jump the hay bales, fences and the hot oven; slide under the carving knives the farmer throws; duck into a haystack with H and let him run straight past you. Corn cobs distract him, golden feathers slow him right down, and a slice of pumpkin pie buys you a life back. Three lives, one farmer who never stops gaining — get caught and you are dinner.

Controls: Space / ↑ / W / click / tap — jump. ↓ / S — slide. H — hide in a haystack. P — pause, M — mute. The HUD shows score, distance, lives, the stage you are in, and how close the farmer is.

The turkey, the farmer, the farm, the barn and the kitchen are all drawn for this game, and the gobbles, the jump boing and the little banjo loop are synthesised live with WebAudio — no audio files, no network: every file the game loads sits under `game/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network.
